02 — Who We Target
Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Newronium's own page says it best: "It makes sense if you lead a company at scale with fragmented data, need a single source of truth, or want to modernize infra for AI." Build every Apollo filter around this sentence.

Company Firmographics

  • Revenue: €10M – €300M (companies "at scale" with real data volume)
  • Employees: 100 – 2,000
  • Funding: Series B–D, or profitable scaleup / SME
  • Multiple departments generating data (ops, finance, marketing, product)
  • Recently hired a Data / Analytics / AI role (intent signal)
  • Posted Data Engineer, BI Analyst, or ML Engineer jobs in last 90 days

Priority Industries

  • Financial Services & Fintech (high data volume, regulatory pressure)
  • Retail & E-commerce (demand forecasting, churn, inventory)
  • Health & MedTech (reporting, compliance, patient data)
  • Logistics & Supply Chain (real-time ops data)
  • SaaS / B2B Tech (product analytics, revenue intelligence)
  • Manufacturing (Industry 4.0 / predictive maintenance)
  • Insurance (risk modelling, claims automation)

Decision Makers (Primary Buyers)

  • CEO / Co-founder (when data = strategic bottleneck)
  • Chief Data Officer (CDO) — owns the mandate
  • Chief Technology Officer (CTO) — owns the infrastructure
  • VP / Head of Data & Analytics
  • Chief Digital / Transformation Officer
  • COO (when "slow decisions" is the pain framing)

Champions & Influencers

  • Data Engineers (frustrated by broken pipelines)
  • BI Analysts (whose dashboards nobody uses)
  • Product Managers owning data products
  • CFO (ROI framing: "Less time wasted, more predictability")
  • IT Director overseeing cloud migration

Intent / Trigger Signals (Apollo + Clay filters)

  • Job postings: Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, BI Lead (90 days)
  • Recent funding round — budget just unlocked
  • New CDO / CTO / CIO hire — they want to make an impact fast
  • Company mentioned "data strategy" or "AI" in press / LinkedIn
  • Tech stack includes legacy BI (Tableau without modern warehouse)
  • Company has 3+ different data tools with no central warehouse

Disqualifiers (Skip in Apollo)

  • Already has a mature, in-house data team (5+ data engineers)
  • Pre-revenue startup — no budget, wrong stage
  • Public sector — long cycles, procurement friction
  • Pure professional services (consultancies, law firms) — not data-driven by nature
  • Engaged a direct data consultancy competitor in last 12 months

06 — The 80% Show Rate
Meeting Attendance Optimization

The industry average show rate is 55–65%. Getting to 80%+ requires a deliberate pre-meeting protocol, not just calendar reminders.

The 6-Touch Pre-Meeting Protocol

T–0

Booking Confirmation

Calendly auto-sends. Include a 1-page "What to expect" PDF + a relevant case study PDF. Prime their expectations immediately.

T–48h

Personalized Loom Video

Record a 90-second video referencing their company, their role, and the specific problem you'll discuss. Send via email. "Here's what I'll cover Thursday."

T–24h

Industry Case Study

Send via email: "Ahead of tomorrow — here's how we solved [their pain] for [company in their sector]. This gives us a starting point."

T–4h

SMS / WhatsApp Reminder

"Looking forward to our call today at [time]. Here's the Zoom link: [link]. Reply RESCHEDULE if the time no longer works." Reduces ghost rate by 20–30%.

T–10min

Manual Ping

A short LinkedIn message or email: "We're on in 10 minutes — here's the meeting link. See you there." Personal touch that signals you're prepared and present.

No Show

No-Show Recovery

Wait 10 minutes, send: "Looks like something came up — no problem. Here are 3 slots for next week: [Calendly link]." Recover 40–60% of no-shows same week.

✅ What Drives Show Rate Up

  • Pre-meeting value delivery (case study, Loom video) — +20–30%
  • Specific time confirmation (not open slot) — +10%
  • SMS/WhatsApp reminder 4h before — +20%
  • Manual personal ping 10 min before — +5–10%
  • Reschedule option in every reminder (reduces ghost) — +10%
  • Short meetings (20–25 min, not 60) — easier to commit to

❌ What Kills Show Rate

  • Only one automated Calendly reminder (default)
  • Generic meeting title ("Discovery Call")
  • No pre-meeting material — prospect forgets why they booked
  • Booking more than 5 days in advance without re-confirming
  • No-show ignored — they never get rescheduled
  • Long gaps between booking and meeting date

09 — The Meeting
Discovery Call Framework (20–25 min)

The goal of the first meeting is NOT to pitch. It's to diagnose pain and earn the right to a second call with a proposal.

Newronium's CTA language: "A structured conversation to reduce noise, focus on the essentials and accelerate with control. 30 min. No commitment." — Use this framing when booking and in all reminders. It positions you as a peer, not a vendor.

0–3 min: Set the Agenda

"I have 30 minutes reserved. My goal is simple — understand where data is creating friction for you, share one or two relevant things we've done for similar companies, and we both decide if a next step makes sense. Structured and direct. Sound good?"

3–15 min: Diagnose the 3 Core Pains

  • "When your leadership meets, are decisions based on data — or is there still a fight about which number is right?"
  • "How much of your data team's time goes into maintaining pipelines vs. producing insights?"
  • "Do you have dashboards that people actually use daily, or are they mostly ignored?"
  • "When a business decision needs to be made — how long does it take to get reliable data to support it?"
  • "Where are you today vs. where you want to be on data maturity in 12 months?"

15–22 min: Relevant Story

Share ONE case study that matches their exact pain. Be specific: company profile, what was broken, what Newronium delivered, the measurable business outcome. Never use a generic slide deck. Reference the outcome in their language: "less uncertainty, more speed."

22–27 min: Qualify (BANT + Fit)

  • "Is this a priority in the next 1–2 quarters, or more of a 2026 initiative?"
  • "Who else would be involved in evaluating this — CTO, CEO?"
  • "Do you have a budget set aside for data infrastructure or AI, or is this still being built?"
  • "Have you worked with external consultancies on data before? What worked and what didn't?"
  • "What does a successful outcome look like for you personally in 6 months?"

27–30 min: Concrete Next Step

Always close with a specific action: "I'll put together a tailored approach document by [date]. Can we book a 30-min follow-up for [specific day] to walk through it together?" Never leave it open. Book the next meeting before hanging up.

Post-Meeting Follow-up (within 2h)

Send an email with: (1) 3-bullet summary of what you heard, (2) the specific Newronium service that matches, (3) a link to a relevant case study, (4) Calendly link for the proposal call. Fast follow-up = credibility signal.
