02 — Who We Target
Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Focus beats volume. One well-researched lead outperforms ten cold blasts. These are the filters for Apollo/Clay prospecting lists.

Company Firmographics

  • Revenue: €5M – €200M (mid-market)
  • Employees: 50 – 1,000
  • Funding: Series A–C or profitable SME
  • Tech stack: modern SaaS tools (signal of data maturity intent)
  • Recently hired a Data / AI role (intent signal)
  • Posted Data Engineer or ML Engineer jobs in past 90 days

Priority Industries

  • Financial Services & Fintech
  • Health & MedTech
  • Retail & E-commerce
  • Logistics & Supply Chain
  • Insurance
  • SaaS / B2B Tech products
  • Manufacturing (Industry 4.0)

Decision Makers (Primary Buyers)

  • Chief Data Officer (CDO)
  • Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
  • VP / Head of Data & Analytics
  • Head of AI / Machine Learning
  • Chief Digital Officer
  • Director of Engineering (Data)

Champions & Influencers

  • Data Engineers (use pain to escalate)
  • Product Managers with data focus
  • BI Analysts frustrated by tooling
  • CFO/COO (when framing ROI)
  • IT Directors overseeing cloud infra

Intent / Trigger Signals

  • Job postings: Data Engineer, ML Engineer, Analytics Lead
  • Recent funding round announcement
  • New C-Suite hire (CDO / CTO / CIO)
  • Company mentioned "AI strategy" in press/earnings
  • Attending data/AI conferences (signal via LinkedIn)
  • Using outdated stack (legacy BI tools, no data warehouse)

Disqualifiers (Do Not Target)

  • Companies with 5+ in-house data scientists already

  • Pure startups pre-product (no budget)

06 — The 80% Show Rate
Meeting Attendance Optimization

The industry average show rate is 55–65%. Getting to 80%+ requires a deliberate pre-meeting protocol, not just calendar reminders.

The 6-Touch Pre-Meeting Protocol

T–0

Booking Confirmation

Calendly auto-sends. Include a 1-page "What to expect" PDF + a relevant case study PDF. Prime their expectations immediately.

T–48h

Personalized Loom Video

Record a 90-second video referencing their company, their role, and the specific problem you'll discuss. Send via email. "Here's what I'll cover Thursday."

T–24h

Industry Case Study

Send via email: "Ahead of tomorrow — here's how we solved [their pain] for [company in their sector]. This gives us a starting point."

T–4h

SMS / WhatsApp Reminder

"Looking forward to our call today at [time]. Here's the Zoom link: [link]. Reply RESCHEDULE if the time no longer works." Reduces ghost rate by 20–30%.

T–10min

Manual Ping

A short LinkedIn message or email: "We're on in 10 minutes — here's the meeting link. See you there." Personal touch that signals you're prepared and present.

No Show

No-Show Recovery

Wait 10 minutes, send: "Looks like something came up — no problem. Here are 3 slots for next week: [Calendly link]." Recover 40–60% of no-shows same week.

✅ What Drives Show Rate Up

  • Pre-meeting value delivery (case study, Loom video) — +20–30%
  • Specific time confirmation (not open slot) — +10%
  • SMS/WhatsApp reminder 4h before — +20%
  • Manual personal ping 10 min before — +5–10%
  • Reschedule option in every reminder (reduces ghost) — +10%
  • Short meetings (20–25 min, not 60) — easier to commit to

❌ What Kills Show Rate

  • Only one automated Calendly reminder (default)
  • Generic meeting title ("Discovery Call")
  • No pre-meeting material — prospect forgets why they booked
  • Booking more than 5 days in advance without re-confirming
  • No-show ignored — they never get rescheduled
  • Long gaps between booking and meeting date

09 — The Meeting
Discovery Call Framework (20–25 min)

The goal of the first meeting is NOT to pitch. It's to diagnose pain and earn the right to a second call with a proposal.

0–3 min: Set the Agenda

"I have 20 minutes reserved. I'd like to understand what's happening on your data/AI side, share a couple of relevant things we've done, and we'll both decide if a next step makes sense. Sound good?"

3–12 min: Diagnose Pain

  • "What's your biggest data challenge right now?"
  • "What does your current data stack look like?"
  • "Where is your team spending the most time that they shouldn't be?"
  • "What would it mean for the business if that was solved?"
  • "What have you already tried?"

12–18 min: Relevant Story

Share ONE case study that maps directly to their pain. Be specific: company size, problem, what you did, measurable result. Never share a generic pitch deck.

18–22 min: Qualify

  • "Is this something you're looking to solve in the next quarter?"
  • "Who else would be involved in this decision?"
  • "Do you have budget allocated, or is this still exploratory?"
  • "What does success look like for you in 6 months?"

22–25 min: Next Step

Always close with a concrete next step: "I'll send you a tailored proposal by [specific date]. Can we book a 30-min follow-up for [specific day]?" Never leave it as "I'll be in touch."

Post-Meeting (within 2h)

Email with: summary of what you heard, the specific outcome you'd deliver, and Calendly link for the proposal review call. Speed of follow-up = professionalism signal.
